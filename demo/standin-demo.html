The rule the stand-in runs

No commands, no control room. Your own messages are the only signal it watches. These four checks decide everything above.

01 · trigger

A contact writes.

02 · presence

If you've typed anywhere recently, you're present — the stand-in stays out.

03 · handoff

If you've typed in this thread, it's yours — it stays quiet here.

04 · answer

Otherwise it answers in your voice, in your thread.

The one thing this demo makes visible: in undisclosed mode the contact's phone looks identical whether you or the stand-in typed — the difference exists only in the right-hand panel, which the contact never sees. Flip Disclosure to labelled and the same reply arrives tagged, which is the position every sanctioned autoresponder takes (a starred Threema ID, a WhatsApp Business away-message, an email vacation reply).
